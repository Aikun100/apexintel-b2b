## 4. Product C: Custom Intelligence Research

Bespoke prospecting campaigns mined directly by our crawler fleet based on client-defined industry, geographic, and diagnostic criteria.

### 4.1 Volume & Pricing Matrix
- **Minimum Engagement:** $299.00
- **Base Rate:** $0.50 per verified record
- **Enrichment Addons:**
  - Mobile Speed TTFB Latency Audit: +$0.08 / record
  - Retargeting Pixel Diagnostics: +$0.08 / record
  - Direct Owner Mobile Line: +$0.12 / record

### 4.2 Transparent Fulfillment Lifecycle
1. `Submitted`: Customer submits request and criteria; order queued.
2. `Reviewing`: Scope and feasibility confirmed by senior data analyst.
3. `Mining`: Automated crawler compilation and DNS MX mailbox handshake.
4. `Delivered`: Verified records delivered directly into customer workspace with downloadable CSV/XLSX.
