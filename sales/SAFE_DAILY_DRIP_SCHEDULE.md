## ⚙️ How to Send: 3 Recommended Deployment Paths

### Option 1: Browser-Assisted Direct Gmail (Current Method — Best for Precision)
- **Tool:** Google Chrome Web UI
- **Process:** Open Gmail in Chrome, copy/paste the personalized pitch from `output/MASS_AGENCY_DISPATCH_SHEET.md`.
- **Spacing:** Send 1 email every 4–6 minutes while doing normal browsing.
- **Advantage:** Maximum human touch, 0% spam risk, highest possible deliverability.

### Option 2: Google Sheets + "Yet Another Mail Merge" (YAMM) / Mailmeteor (Fastest Free Setup)
- **Tool:** Google Sheets with [Mailmeteor](https://mailmeteor.com) or [YAMM](https://yamm.com) add-on.
- **Process:**
  1. Open Google Sheets -> Import `output/MASS_AGENCY_BUYERS_120.csv`.
  2. Create a Gmail draft with placeholders: `Hi {{Agency Name}}`, `{{Personalized Pitch}}`.
  3. Set sending speed to: **20 emails per day with 120-second randomized delays**.
- **Advantage:** Automated 1-click batch sending without leaving your Gmail ecosystem.

### Option 3: Dedicated Cold Outreach Engine (For Scaling to 500–1,000+ Monthly)
- **Platforms:** [Instantly.ai](https://instantly.ai) or [Smartlead.ai](https://smartlead.ai)
- **Process:**
  1. Register 2-3 secondary sending domains (e.g., `aaronagbas-intel.com`, `getapexintel.com`).
  2. Connect to Instantly with auto-warmup enabled (14 days).
  3. Upload `output/MASS_AGENCY_BUYERS_120.csv`.
  4. Launch an automated 3-step sequence (Touch 1 -> Value Follow-up -> Breakup).
- **Advantage:** Uncapped scale, zero risk to your personal email, automated follow-up sequences.
