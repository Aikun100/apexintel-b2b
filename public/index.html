Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about datasets, verification, purchases, and delivery.

What exact fields are included in each intelligence dataset? ▼

Every record contains 9 core fields: Company Legal Name, Physical Metro Location, Trade Specialization, 100% DNS MX-Verified Decision-Maker Email, Direct Phone Number, Measured Mobile Speed Latency (TTFB in ms), Ad Retargeting Pixel Audit Status (Meta/Google), Detected CMS / Host Technology, and a Pre-Audited Actionable Pitch Angle.

How does the Zero-Duplicate Ledger protect my territory? ▼

Whenever a customer acquires a pack (e.g. Texas Contractors), our SHA-256 deduplication ledger assigns those specific records to that agency's profile and flags them as claimed in that rotation. This prevents dozens of agencies from spamming the exact same contractors simultaneously.

How does payment work, and how soon do I receive my files? ▼

You can pay securely with any major Credit/Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, or Google Pay via Stripe Checkout. Once authorized, your dataset is permanently unlocked in your Client Dashboard for instant .XLSX download via 15-minute presigned S3 URLs.

Are these emails compliant with B2B cold outreach laws (CAN-SPAM)? ▼

Yes. All records represent corporate and commercial entities collected from public commercial sources. Under the US CAN-SPAM Act, B2B cold emailing is 100% legal provided that you include an accurate subject line, your physical mailing address, and a clear opt-out mechanism.

Can I request a custom dataset for a specific state or niche? ▼

Yes! Use our Custom Extraction Builder above. You can specify any target industry, US states or cities, requested volume (from 100 to 2,500+ records), and enrichment signals. Our automated crawler compiles and delivers custom orders within 4 to 12 hours.
