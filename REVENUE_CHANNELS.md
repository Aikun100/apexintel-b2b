# Revenue Channels & Monetization Playbooks

## Channel 1: High-Value Lead Pack Sales (Gumroad / Whop / B2B Forums)
* **Product:** Pack of 200–500 niche-specific, verified leads with phone numbers, emails, and tech stacks.
* **Pricing:** \$49 – \$199 per pack.
* **Top Niches:**
  1. Solar Installation Companies in CA/FL/TX
  2. Dental & MedSpa Clinics with missing ad pixels
  3. E-commerce Brands using Shopify with poor mobile speeds
* **Listing Copy Blueprint:**
  * *Headline:* "500 Verified [Niche] Leads with Direct Emails & Tech Audit"
  * *Deliverable:* Formatted Excel file with DNS-verified emails and direct phone numbers.

## Channel 2: Upwork / Fiverr Gigs (Immediate Client Cash Flow)
* **Gig Title:** "I will scrape and deliver verified B2B leads with email validation"
* **Pricing Structure:**
  * *Basic (\$25):* 100 leads + Email & Phone
  * *Standard (\$75):* 500 leads + Email, Phone, Socials
  * *Premium (\$150):* 1,000 leads + Full Website Audit (CMS, pixels, speed flaws)
* **Delivery Time:** Under 2 hours using our automated pipeline.

## Channel 3: Cold Audit Outreach (High-Ticket Retainers)
* **Strategy:** Use our auditor to find companies with missing SSL, broken mobile viewports, or missing Facebook Pixels.
* **Cold Email Template:**
